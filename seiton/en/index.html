Why Seiton?

Capture first. Weave structure later.

Seiton is not a notes app or a task manager. It is a relationship map for messy work.

I

Drop things where they are

Catch the fragment before you know where it belongs. The inbox can be organized later, with AI beside you.

II

Relations become a star chart

after, part_of, related. Direction carries meaning, so context returns faster than through folder trees.

III

Your AI, on your PC

AI is optional: a CLI you configure, manual copy/paste, or none. No telemetry and no hidden cloud sync.

Three Scales

Galaxy, system, star. Change the grain of attention.

Start from the whole, move into a center, then return to the next concrete action.

Galaxy

See tag clusters and large flows at once. Scattered work begins to show its regions.

System

Place one item at the center and read before, after, parent, parts, and orbiting context by direction.

Star

Use the desk to recover today's routes, recent work, and inbox items before taking the next step.

Privacy

Privacy principles

Not a black box, but a tool on your own desk. You decide which places it may read.

Self-hosted and fully local
A 127.0.0.1 local server and SQLite on your own machine. No bundled cloud sync.

Reads only approved places
Only registered folders are read. Choose names-only or content-reading per root.

No telemetry
Usage is not sent anywhere. AI calls happen only through your configured CLI or manual copy flow.
